This master’s thesis addresses the digital transformation of scientific publishing in the
field of social sciences and humanities in Spain, focusing on the implementation of the
XML JATS standard. It proposes a three-pronged solution that integrates an analysis of the
current situation, the design of an XML-first workflow, and the development of a Python
tool to automate the front-end tagging of articles. This practical approach, complemented by
support materials (XSLT, CSS, Lua filter) and validated with a real case study from Ediciones
Complutense, demonstrates the feasibility of implementing semantic editing processes that
can be shared and reused by the university and scientific community in line with the ideas
advocated by open science (open access). The project, available on GitHub, highlights the
need to promote hybrid profiles, such as the digital humanist editor, capable of building
bridges between technology, the humanities, and publishing.
